### Explanation

Reported speech with a past reporting verb backshifts will/won't to would/wouldn't, and 'tomorrow' changes to 'the following day' or 'the next day'. B keeps the direct-speech forms won't and tomorrow with no backshift. C wrongly changes 'won't be' to 'wasn't' (loses the future-in-the-past meaning) and uses 'the day before', which refers to the past. D has the correct 'wouldn't be' but 'the day before' points to the past, while the workshop is in the future relative to Monday.

### Solution

1. Reporting verb 'told' is past, so backshift: won't be able -> wouldn't be able.
2. 'Tomorrow' said on Monday, reported at a later time, becomes 'the following day' (or 'the next day').
3. Combine: wouldn't be / the following day.
