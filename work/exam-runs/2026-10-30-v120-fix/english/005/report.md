### Explanation

The fixed correlative pattern is 'Hardly ... when' (also 'No sooner ... than'). B. than is paired with 'no sooner', not 'hardly'. C. then is not a conjunction and cannot join the clauses. D. while expresses simultaneous or contrasting actions, not one action immediately following another.

### Solution

1. The structure 'Hardly had + subject + past participle' is an inversion meaning 'as soon as'.
2. Hardly is followed by 'when' to link the two past events.
3. So the blank is 'when'.
