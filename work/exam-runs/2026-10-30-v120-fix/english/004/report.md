### Explanation

b introduces the topic and time (Last year). d explains why she chose it, and 'at first' sets up a change. a begins with 'However' and 'soon realised', which contrasts with the easy choice in d. e starts with 'Because of this', so it must follow a, the difficulty that caused the training. c begins with 'Now, after finishing the courses', so it must come last, after e mentions the courses. Other orders break these links: a cannot precede d because 'However' needs a prior positive idea, and c cannot come before e because the courses have not yet been mentioned.

### Solution

1. b opens the story: Linh decides to become a primary school teacher (Last year).
2. d gives the reason for the choice: she loved children, so it seemed easy at first.
3. a contrasts with d using However: the job needed more patience than expected.
4. e follows as the result of a (Because of this): she attended extra training courses.
5. c closes with Now, after finishing the courses, she feels it was the most rewarding decision.
