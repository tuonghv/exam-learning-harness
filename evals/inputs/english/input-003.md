## Passage

Many students believe that studying late at night helps them remember more. However, researchers at a university in Canada found the opposite. They asked two groups of students to learn the same list of fifty words. The first group studied for two hours before midnight and then slept for eight hours. The second group studied the list after midnight and slept for only four hours. The next morning, the first group remembered an average of thirty-eight words, while the second group remembered only twenty-six. The researchers explained that sleep helps the brain store new information. They advise students to review their lessons in the evening and to avoid staying up late before an exam.
