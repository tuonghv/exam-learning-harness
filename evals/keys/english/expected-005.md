Correct answer: A. when

### Solution

1. "Hardly had she entered" is an inverted structure (auxiliary before subject) after a negative adverb.
2. The fixed pattern is "Hardly + had + subject + past participle ... when + past simple" (two events in quick succession).
3. So the linking word is "when".

### Explanation

- A. when: "Hardly ... when" is the correct pair - correct.
- B. than: "than" belongs to "No sooner ... than"; it is the common trap because both patterns mean the same - wrong.
- C. then: "then" is an adverb of time, not a conjunction joining the two clauses - wrong.
- D. while: "while" means during the same time, not one event right after another - wrong.
