## Requirement
WHEN an exam is cross-checked, the system SHALL solve it through two independent inputs with the unchanged solving engine (route A: text from native extraction or refine; route B: the page images, no transcript) and compare the final answers per question: equal answers -> `agree`; different, missing or declined -> `needs_review` with both answers and the reason. Agreement is evidence, not proof of correctness. Off by default; real calls only with `--real` and the user's go; dry-run shows call count and cap. Supersedes the symbol-level diff design (user accepted the lean plan, RAW-00074).
