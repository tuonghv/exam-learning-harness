## Requirement
WHEN extraction of a math question is finished, the verification stage SHALL run independent checks and set `verification.status`: (a) route agreement (native/OCR text vs vision-refined text) after math normalization; (b) symbol-level checks on signs, exponents, fractions, radicals, option letters and count, geometric labels (A..Z) against the other route and the crop; (c) structural checks (consecutive numbering, 4 options, balanced brackets/LaTeX parses); any disagreement, missing region or low-confidence OCR yields `needs_review` with the exact reasons, never a guess; `verified` requires every configured check to pass.
