## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
WHEN a question asks for an approximate or nearest value (gần nhất, approximately, closest), the solver SHALL compute the plausible readings (e.g. start vs end of period) and choose the option nearest to the computed value instead of rejecting a non-exact match. Evidence: DGNL 2026 Math 63: the solver computed 19.855 million, rejected it because no option matched exactly, and chose another reading (19.975 million); the key option is the nearest to 19.856 million.
