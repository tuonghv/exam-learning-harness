## Requirement
WHEN a scanned PDF or image is read by OCR (tesseract), the engine SHALL recover usable question text (words not glued together, page furniture not leaked into stems) and SHALL mark a question whose OCR text is doubtful (confidence, glued words, character errors, leaked furniture) so it is declined or reviewed instead of silently sent to the solver. Evidence (TASK-046, synthetic families F1-F8 through tesseract, 200 questions, 2-page-scale fixtures): 162 questions differ from gold only by lost spaces, 6 have real errors (F8 character errors, a leaked page header), and all 200 reached the solver with 0 markers.
