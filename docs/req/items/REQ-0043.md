## Requirement
WHEN a question (its stem, shared passage, figure/table, options) spans two or more PDF pages or image files, the engine SHALL assemble the WHOLE question before it is solved, however many pages it spans, for every source type the engine reads: PDF, page images (pictures) and DOCX (user, RAW-00145); a question is never solved from a fragment, and a fragment that cannot be completed is reported as `missing_text`, not guessed. The extraction skill SHALL teach this rule to the agents (user, RAW-00144: a known defect must not stay and come back). Evidence (REQ-0042 cross-check of the real DGNL sample): printed 66 lost its question sentence and the weighted graph (top of the next page), 10 and 23 lost passage paragraphs, 21-25 lost paragraph 5 and the attribution; three of these four were declined by the solver.
