## Requirement
WHEN a question's stem sentence, figure or shared-passage paragraphs continue onto the next PDF page, the refine stage SHALL attach that continuation to the right question, so that no question loses text or figure content at a page break. Evidence (REQ-0042 cross-check of the real DGNL sample): printed 66 lost its question sentence and the weighted graph (top of the next page), 10 and 23 lost passage paragraphs, 21-25 lost paragraph 5 and the attribution; three of these four were declined by the solver.
