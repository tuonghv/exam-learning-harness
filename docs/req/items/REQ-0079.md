## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
WHEN a solve run has answers of mixed quality, the engine SHALL separate likely-correct from likely-wrong answers with a cheap independent check, so that status is not UNCERTAIN for all of them. Evidence: DGNL 2026 run 3 scored 111 of 120 correct; all 120 were UNCERTAIN, PASS never fired, and the 5 wrong answers carried only low or medium solver uncertainty (work/TASK-037/evidence/dgnl-score.txt).
