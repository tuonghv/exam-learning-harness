## Requirement
WHEN an exam PDF has question numbers that are not consecutive from 1 (a part or page selection, numbering that restarts per section, or 31..60 style ranges), ingest SHALL still segment the questions it finds and keep their printed numbers, and SHALL report in the warnings which numbers were missing, instead of emitting `segmentation_skipped` with no questions. Found by TASK-036 tester D2 (ids 1 then 150 gave no questions).
