## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
WHEN the opt-in boundary AI gets a reply that omits a requested line, a tampered cache entry or duplicate window ids, or estimates/traces costs, and WHEN a refined slice is gated by verification, the harness SHALL treat incomplete or corrupt replies as misses, report estimates and per-call costs correctly, keep a passage line out of option D under a hint, and gate a slice by the whole document's verification status.

## Acceptance

- [ ] AC2: a cache entry of the wrong shape (`[1]`, `"0.9"`, `{"a":1}`) is a miss, never a TypeError/ValueError (118-F003).

- [ ] AC7: a refined slice whose own questions lack `verification` is still gated when the document has verification data (117-F006).
