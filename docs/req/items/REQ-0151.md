## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
WHEN a numeric answer is compared with a numeric key, the scorer SHALL accept it only when it differs by strictly less than
half a unit of the key's last digit (key `5`: `5.49` correct, `5.5` and `4.5` wrong), and SHALL NOT accept a decimal
answer for a fraction key (`4.67` vs `14/3` wrong; exact fractions such as `28/6` stay correct).
