## Requirement
WHEN questions of one group share an instruction ("Questions 31-35: Choose a suitable word or phrase (A, B, C, or D) to fill in each blank."), the exam text SHALL carry that instruction in every question of the group (it does today and must stay), but section headings such as `PHẦN 1: ...` or `1.2. TIẾNG ANH` SHALL NOT be repeated inside each question's stem: they appear once at group level (exam text section line or the review pack subject header). Found in the user's review of printed 31-32 (RAW-00160): each question is already separate with options A-D, the group instruction is present, but the section heading is repeated in the stem of every question of the group.
