## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
WHEN the free text path (ingest --exam-out without --refined) writes a Multiple Choice exam, the ingest SHALL mark a
question that has no A-D options with `[Incomplete: missing_text options_missing]` so the engine declines that one question
instead of refusing the whole file; and WHEN a group instruction (`Questions 36-40: ...`) wraps onto a next line that
continues the sentence, the exam text SHALL keep the whole instruction as the group instruction, not as the passage.
The DGNL guide SHALL describe the text path (section slicing, pinned --model).
