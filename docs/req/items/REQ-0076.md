## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
The scorer SHALL keep the sign of a final answer when comparing with the key: an answer that differs from the key only by a leading minus (ASCII or U+2212) SHALL NOT match. Evidence: TASK-067 tester found that scorer._strip drops the leading `-` (pre-existing): `-sqrt(2)` matches key `sqrt(2)` and `sqrt(2)` matches key `-sqrt(2)` (work/TASK-067/handoff/test.md). This is a false-pass class and can inflate official scores.
