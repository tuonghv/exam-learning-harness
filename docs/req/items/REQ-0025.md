## Requirement
The system SHALL provide a gold set of representative math questions (digital PDF, scanned, Vietnamese diacritics, formulas, tables, diagrams, multi-column) with hand-checked expected question JSON, and a command that reports exact question-level accuracy overall and per field (text, formula, options, figure refs), plus the verified-subset precision (fraction of `verified` questions that are exactly right), with a confidence interval; reports SHALL state sample size and SHALL NOT claim any rate the sample cannot support.
