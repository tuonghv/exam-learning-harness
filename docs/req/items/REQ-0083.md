## Requirement
<!-- Clear, testable statement in one language (English preferred). EARS: "WHEN <trigger>, the <system> SHALL <response>." -->
(1) WHEN a model reply holds several JSON objects (a self-correction after the first), the engine SHALL use the last object that validates instead of marking all questions malformed. (2) WHEN the text answer and the page-image answer both exist and differ, the engine SHALL settle it with the adjudication step (or a documented tie-break) and record the reason, and SHALL NOT silently keep the text answer nor silently replace an answered text round. (3) WHEN a question is self-contained but references a table or figure of an earlier question, the engine SHALL NOT flag it missing_text. Evidence: DGNL 2025 scoring, work/TASK-073/evidence/dgnl2025-score.txt: 107 and 117 lost to (2), 59 flipped by (2), 9 English questions marked malformed because of (1), Science 111 sent to the image call by (3).
